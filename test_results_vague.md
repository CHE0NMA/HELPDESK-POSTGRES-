### ✅ Q5: What's the school's philosophy about education?

**Answer:**

The school's philosophy about education is to develop the whole person - spiritually, mentally, physically, and socially - and prepare students for meaningful service to God, the community, and the nation, as stated in the Declaration of Principles on page 14. It's also about reflecting Christ in the total lifestyle of the faculty and students for useful and fruitful citizenship. The philosophy aims to prepare students to be better citizens of this world and the world to come.

### ✅ Q9: How many points do we start with each semester?

**Answer:**

According to the Student's Behavioral Code on page 16, at the beginning of the semester, each student is given a merit of 30 points. This is part of the point system that's at the heart of the discipline process on a semestral basis. So, you start with 30 points each semester.

### ✅ Q10: What's a first degree offense worth?

**Answer:**

A first-degree offense is punishable by either rendering labor for free or by paying a fine to be determined by the Students Disciplinary Committee, and it's worth 5 demerits. You can find this info in the First-Degree Offense section on page 17.

---

### ✅ Q11: How bad is a second-degree offense in demerits?

**Answer:**

A second-degree offense is pretty serious and can result in 15 demerits, as stated in the Second-Degree Offense section on page 18. This is in addition to possible labor or fines that don't exceed 1,500 pesos. It's definitely something to take seriously and try to avoid.

### ✅ Q14: What kind of things count as medium offenses?

### ✅ Q15: What are the really serious offenses?

**Answer:**

According to the Third-Degree Offense section on page 19, really serious offenses include things like immorality, forgery, possession and consumption of prohibited substances, fighting, serious vandalism, theft, and more, which can lead to withdrawal or dismissal from school for the semester or the remainder of the school year. These offenses are considered severe and can result in serious consequences. They are worth 30 demerits.

### ✅ Q18: What are we actually allowed to wear on campus?

### ✅ Q20: What happens if you get too many demerits, like 20+?

**Answer:**

If you get 20 demerits or more, less than one month before the end of the term, you'll be placed on probation immediately, according to the disciplinary measures outlined on page 14. This means you won't be able to hold any office or responsibility during the midterm or semester end. The Student Disciplinary Committee will review your probation status and it may be maintained or lifted based on recommendations.
