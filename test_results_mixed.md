### ✅ Q58 [Vague]: Any policy from MORPHE of 2008?

Here’s a quick rundown of the MORPHE 2008 policies that are highlighted in the handbook:Authority to Maintain Discipline (Sec. 102)  • Schools must keep order on campus and in the immediate surroundings.  • They can also act on students outside campus or after hours when:    1. A school policy/regulation is broken, or    2. The misconduct harms the student’s status or the school’s reputation.Authority to Set Rules (Sec. 103)  • Each private higher‑ed institution can create reasonable norms, rules, and regulations for discipline and attendance.  • Rules must list specific offenses and their penalties, but students can still be held liable for broadly worded offenses that cover related wrongdoings.  • Rules become effective once they’re promulgated and notified to students.Disciplinary Sanctions (Sec. 104)  • Officials may impose appropriate, reasonable measures for minor offenses they witness.  • No cruel or physically harmful punishment is allowed.Administrative Actions (Sec. 105)  • Schools may file administrative actions against students who violate disciplinary rules.  • Such actions must follow the minimum due‑process standards set out in the manual.Hope that gives you a clear picture of the key MORPHE 2008 provisions!  (More detail is on page 15.)</div>
