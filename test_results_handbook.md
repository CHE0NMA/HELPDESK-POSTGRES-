### ✅ Q17: What counts as a serious or third-degree offense?

**Answer:**

According to the handbook, a serious or third-degree offense includes things like immorality, forgery, and possession or consumption of harmful substances, as well as other cases like physical injury, theft, and vandalism, as listed in Excerpt 2 on page 21. ### ✅ Q18: What happens if you get 20 or more demerits?

**Answer:**

If you get 20 or more demerits, you'll be placed on probation immediately, as stated in the F. DISCIPLINARY MEASURES section on page 14. This means you won't be able to hold any office or responsibility during the midterm or semester end. According to the rules, probation will last for one semester, and if it's not lifted, you might face denial of admission for the following term.
