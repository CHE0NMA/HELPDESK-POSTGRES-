Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026 · Philippine Advent College (PAC) Help Desk

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the PAC Help Desk chatbot ("PAC Bot") collects, uses, and protects information when you use this website or chat service. PAC Bot is provided by Philippine Advent College to help students and visitors find answers to school-related questions — including enrollment, fees, registrar processes, scholarships, and the Student Handbook.

Information We Collect

To operate the chat service and improve its answers, we may collect:

  • Chat messages — the questions you type and the responses PAC Bot gives, stored against a temporary session ID.
  • Session & device data — a randomly generated session identifier, IP address, and basic browser information, used for rate‑limiting and abuse prevention.
  • Feedback — thumbs up/down ratings, star ratings, and optional comments you submit about a response.
  • Usage statistics — aggregate counts such as number of visitors and popular questions, used to improve the FAQ and Handbook content.

We do not require you to create an account or provide your name, student number, or email address to use the chat.

How Responses Are Generated

PAC Bot uses a combination of a curated FAQ database, the official PAC Student Handbook, and a third‑party AI language model to understand your question and draft an answer. Your message text may be sent to that AI provider solely to generate a response — it is not used by the provider to identify you personally, and PAC does not send your name or contact details along with it.

Data Retention

Chat history is kept for your current session so PAC Bot can understand follow‑up questions, and may be retained in our database for quality review and to improve future answers. Rate‑limit and session counters stored in your browser (localStorage) are cleared automatically or when you start a New Chat.

Who Can See Your Messages

Chat logs, satisfaction ratings, and reported ("thumbs down") answers are visible only to authorized PAC Help Desk administrators, who use them to correct inaccurate answers and expand the FAQ/Handbook coverage. We do not sell, rent, or share your chat data with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

Cookies & Local Storage

We use your browser's local storage (not tracking cookies) to remember your session ID and message count, so the chat works smoothly across page reloads. This data stays on your device and is not used for advertising.

Your Choices

  • Start a New Chat at any time to clear your current conversation context.
  • Skip the feedback/rating prompts — they are always optional.
  • Contact the PAC Registrar's Office or IT Office if you'd like previously submitted feedback removed.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or how your data is handled? Reach out to the PAC administration office during regular business hours, or raise it through the chat itself using the 👎 feedback option so it reaches our review queue.
