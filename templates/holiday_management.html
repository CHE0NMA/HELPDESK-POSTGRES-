How Holidays, Exceptions & Emergency Closures Work
🌐Global Holidays apply to all departments unless overridden. Bot answers "Is there class?" using this as the baseline.
🔀Department Exceptions override globals for a specific department.
🚨Emergency Closures (heavy rain, typhoon, etc.) override everything and are shown with severity levels.
⚡Rule of Specificity: Emergency > Exception > Global > Default Schedule.
